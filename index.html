How do I remove GPS location from photos?

Simply drag your image into PureScrub. Our engine strips EXIF tags, including GPS coordinates, ensuring your home or office location remains private before you share on social media.

Is PureScrub free for commercial use?

Yes. We believe privacy is a right. PureScrub is a 100% free web utility for journalists, photographers, and everyday users to clean photo metadata instantly.

Yes. PureScrub is a progressive web utility that works in any mobile browser. You can scrub metadata directly from your phone's camera roll without installing an app.

What is EXIF data?

EXIF data is a hidden file inside your image that stores camera settings, timestamps, and sensitive location markers. Our tool purges this data completely.
